# T-121 — Design the approve verdict (ADR only)

## What was done

ADR 258 in `docs/decisions.md` answers Steps 2 to 5 of the task card:

- **Write path (Step 2).** A bearer route `POST /api/tasks/<id>/approve`
  writes the `approve` verdict line, pinned to the reviewed tip, then an
  `approve` action. A stale verdict is refused under the base lock.
- **How the dispatcher learns (Step 3).** Through the action queue, which is
  neither Option A nor Option B as posed.
- **Credential (Step 4).** ADR 78's, unchanged. Step 5 needs only its own
  function and switch.
- **Failure paths (Step 5).** The ADR's outcome table gives the task status,
  the action status and the comment for each case.

`docs/plans/review.md` step 4 is marked *Designed 2026-10-21* and points at
ADR 258.

## Why this way

The action queue is the console's existing channel to the side holding the
Docker socket and the credential (ADR 142, C-12), so the design adds no
second channel and no listener.

## Ruled out

Listed in the ADR's *Ruled out*: polling `comments.jsonl`, a service signal,
merging in the board service, merging at a newer tip, and taking ADR 200's
lock.
