# T-081 — `phase` in the task file's frontmatter (board-service step 6)

Decisions: `docs/decisions.md` ADR 126. It covers the key on `TaskFile`, a
role that is written and cleared with the lock rather than by writers of its
own, what clears the role a killed dispatcher left, and the api serving it.

## What was built

- `dispatcher/context_transfer.py`: `TaskFile.phase`. It is read from the
  frontmatter, and written after `command_verdicts` only when set, so a file
  without one keeps its bytes.
- `dispatcher/tasks.py`: `acquire_lock(..., phase=None)` writes the role with
  `owner` and `heartbeat`, replacing any earlier one. `release_stale_lock`,
  `handoff` and `mark_blocked` clear it. The module docstring says what clears
  a stale one.
- `dispatcher/dispatcher.py`: `dispatch_phase` passes `phase=role` to its
  `acquire_lock`. Its exits already drop the lock, through
  `release_stale_lock` or through the caller's `handoff`.
- `observability/api/app.py`: `_task` serves `phase` on `/api/tasks` and
  `/api/tasks/<id>`, `null` when the file has none.
- Tests:
  - `tests/dispatcher/test_tasks.py` has a *phase (ADR 126)* section. It
    covers:
    - acquire writing the role, and refresh keeping it;
    - a new acquire replacing it, and one with no role leaving none;
    - every lock-dropping writer clearing it;
    - `set_resolved_debt` and `mark_merged` keeping it;
    - a file without a role keeping its bytes.
  - `tests/dispatcher/test_dispatcher.py` covers:
    - the role written while each of the five roles runs;
    - the role cleared when a phase fails or raises;
    - `reap_expired_locks` clearing a killed dispatcher's role.
  - `tests/observability/test_api.py`: both routes serve the role written by
    `acquire_lock` and `null` after `handoff`. The exact-row tests now carry
    `phase`.
- Docs: ADR 126.

## Why this way

- With the lock, because every way out of a phase already drops the lock.
  The role then needs no call site of its own that could be missed.
- A stale role is the reaper's, because a stale role is a stale lock. A
  separate lifetime would give a second answer to "is this task held".
- The api serves the file as it is. `lock_expired: true` beside a role is
  how a reader knows the dispatcher died.

## Pending

- The board's role lanes (step 8, front).
- The phase's start time, model and duration (ADR 28's list). Only the role
  is written.
- `reap_expired_locks` reaps only `in_progress`. A phase killed on a task in
  another status keeps its stale lock and role until `dispatch
  release-account` or the next lock. That predates this task.

## Ruled out

- A `set_phase` / `clear_phase` pair called around each phase.
- Hiding or clearing a stale role in the api.
- A startup sweep that clears every role.
- The role in `state_machine` instead of the task file.
- A board card (ADR 26).

## Proposed Built note

For step 6 of `docs/plans/board-service.md`:

> **Built 2026-10-18** by T-081 (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 126): `phase` in
> the task file's frontmatter, written by `tasks.acquire_lock` with the lock
> as each phase starts and cleared by every writer that drops the lock
> (`release_stale_lock`, `handoff`, `mark_blocked`); a killed dispatcher's is
> cleared by `reap_expired_locks` at the next cycle or by `dispatch
> release-account`, and until then `lock_expired` is true beside it.
> `/api/tasks` and `/api/tasks/<id>` serve it, `null` when unset. The board's
> role lanes are step 8.

## What the front would change

- `front/src/lib/api/types.ts`: `phase: Role | null` on `Task`.
- The In progress column splits into role lanes by `status === "in_progress"`
  and `phase`.
- An `in_progress` task with `phase: null` is a reaped lock or an old file.
  It needs a place that is not a role lane.
- A task between two phases of one cycle is `pending` with no role, so it
  leaves its lane for that gap.
- A role beside `lock_expired: true` is a dead dispatcher's. Show it as
  stale, not as running.
- The ADR 28 banner about the role being unknown can go. The role filter can
  use `phase`, and the task page can show it.
- `splitStatus` stays unused.
