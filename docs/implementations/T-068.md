# T-068 — the run skill generated in mapping, staged and moved into the mapping commit (skills-adoption step 1)

Decisions: `docs/decisions.md` ADR 115 (the frontmatter stays the only thing a
gate reads, the staging and the move, generated once, `mapping_allowed_tools`
for the cartografo alone, `install` in `MAPPED_KEYS`, the fingerprint left on
`allowed_tools`).

## What was built

The checks, recorded as given (probes ran 2026-10-17 as `claude -p` in
agent-cuenta1, Haiku, `acceptEdits`, the standing allowlist, a toy project; no
probe was repeated for this task):
1. *CLI version: holds.* 2.1.296, pinned at `docker/agent/Dockerfile`
   (`ARG CLAUDE_CODE_VERSION`); the generator needs 2.1.145 or later.
2. *The generator runs under `-p`: holds.* The prompt starts with
   `/run-skill-generator` and may carry instructions after it; the init event
   lists it in `slash_commands` and `skills`.
3. *A write under `.claude/skills/` is refused* (`permission_denials`, nothing
   written); a write to `docs/` succeeds. *Staging works:* told to write the
   skill to `docs/run-skill/run-toy/SKILL.md` instead of
   `.claude/skills/run-toy/SKILL.md`, the generator wrote `SKILL.md` and
   `driver.mjs` there, made no attempt on `.claude/`, and took 16 turns. Its
   one flaw, the skill's text naming the staging path, is now forbidden by the
   prompt. With the standing allowlist only `node --test` ran; `npm test`,
   `node <script>`, `npm --version` and compound commands were refused, and
   the skill marked what it could not verify as unverified.
4. *Bringing a project up in the dind:* not verified (under *Pending*).

The code:
- `dispatcher/project_docs.py`: `mapper_lead()` is the mapper prompt's first
  line, `/run-skill-generator` (`RUN_SKILL_COMMAND`) followed by the staging
  terms, and `dispatcher._role_prompt` puts it before `Role:`. `_MAPPER`
  says the generator runs first and maps after. The lead points it at
  `docs/run-skill/run-<name>/`, with every in-skill path its final
  `.claude/skills/run-<name>/...` one and no mention of the staging dir; a
  refused command is unverified, never guessed. The frontmatter keys come from
  what the generator ran and saw succeed, the docs table gains the skill's
  row, and the budget line covers running out mid-generator. `MAPPED_KEYS`
  gains `install`. New `RUN_SKILL_STAGING`, `RUN_SKILLS_DIR` and
  `move_staged_run_skills(container, workdir)`, which moves each
  `docs/run-skill/run-[a-z0-9-]+/` whole, logs and skips a bad name, a
  non-directory or an existing target, then removes `docs/run-skill/`; with
  nothing staged it runs only the `find`.
- `dispatcher/dispatcher.py`: `_phase_permission_flags(cfg, role)` appends
  `mapping_allowed_tools` for `MAPPER_ROLE` only; its five callers pass the
  role. The mapping branch of `dispatch_phase` calls the move inside the
  `"mapping commit"` `_base_lock`, before `commit_worktree`.
- `dispatcher/config.py`: `mapping_allowed_tools` (default `[]`), a read-only
  `CONFIG_KEYS` row, loaded by `_load_allowed_tools(raw, key)`, which now
  names the key in its errors.
- `config.example.yaml`: the commented, empty `# mapping_allowed_tools: []`
  with its comment (examples `Bash(docker compose*)`, `Bash(npm *)`,
  `Bash(make*)`; the owner fills it per the projects in `projects_root`).
  `config.yaml` is gitignored and lives only in the operator's checkout, not
  in this worktree, so it was not touched; the example is the tracked copy.
- Tests: `tests/dispatcher/test_run_skill_staging.py` (new; the move on a real
  tree, and a real-git mapping commit holding `.claude/skills/run-myproj/`
  and nothing under `docs/run-skill/`); in `test_project_docs.py` the prompt
  and the derived keys, and `read_commands` with a skill present and `test:`
  missing (only `cat docs/README.md` is ever run); in `test_dispatcher.py`
  the move under the lock before the commit, no move for any other role, and
  the flags per role; in `test_config.py` the key's default, load and
  validation. The autouse `fake_git` fixture fakes the move, so no dispatcher
  test reaches docker.
- Docs: ADR 115; the *Built* note under section 1 of
  `docs/plans/skills-adoption.md`.

*The `WRITER_ROLES` readers of `cfg.allowed_tools` stay coherent* by being
left as they are. The two `learnings.harness_fingerprint(cfg.permission_mode,
cfg.allowed_tools, docker_exec.WRITER_ROLES)` calls in `dispatcher.py` (in
`run_task` and in split filing; `cli.py` has a third) fingerprint the grants
a task's cycle phases run under. Those grants are unchanged:
`mapping_allowed_tools` reaches only the cartografo's call, a one-time phase
that writes the docs and the staged skill, so no learning a later phase reads
was earned under a grant it does not hold.

## Why this way

- The move is a fixed dispatcher step because the phase cannot write under
  `.claude/` and must not have its permissions widened for it.
- Removing `docs/run-skill/` after the move means a refused or unmoved copy
  never reaches the commit, whatever the reason it stayed.
- The move runs before the commit and under the same lock, so the skill is in
  the mapping commit and nothing else touches the base in between.

Two more probes, run after review with the owner's go-ahead (Haiku,
`acceptEdits`, `Bash(node --test*)`, 30 turns, toy project):
- *The generator named inside `_MAPPER`:* it never ran. The Skill tool
  refused it as user-invocable only. The mapper mapped anyway, with an empty
  frontmatter and nothing staged. Hence the lead line.
- *The rendered prompt with the lead line first:* success in 23 turns. The
  generator wrote `docs/run-skill/run-toy/SKILL.md` and `smoke.js`, neither
  mentioning `docs/run-skill/`. Then the mapper wrote the three docs, with
  `test: node --test` (seen passing) in the frontmatter and the index's table
  pointing at `.claude/skills/run-toy/SKILL.md`. Refused, as expected with the
  empty `mapping_allowed_tools`: `npm test`, compound commands, the smoke
  driver, and `git add`/`git commit` (the dispatcher commits).

## Pending

- *Check 4:* bringing a project up in the agent's dind under `-p` is not
  verified; no `dind-cuenta1` container exists, and none was started.
- A project's own `.gitignore` could exclude `.claude/`, and the moved skill
  would then be left out of the commit; the dispatcher does not override it.
- The owner fills `mapping_allowed_tools` in the live `config.yaml` and turns
  `mapping_enabled` on; with the list empty the frontmatter will be thin.
- `dispatcher/gates.py` needed no change.

## Ruled out

- Reading the skill from the gates: a gate has no model to read prose.
- Widening the cartografo's permissions to write `.claude/`, or
  `bypassPermissions` for mapping.
- Overwriting an existing `.claude/skills/<dir>/`: it may be the owner's own.
- Folding `mapping_allowed_tools` into the fingerprint: it would mark every
  task's learnings as earned under a grant no cycle phase holds.
