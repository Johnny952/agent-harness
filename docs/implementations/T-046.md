# T-046 — the task detail's usage region

Specification: `docs/plans/front.md` *The usage task: what a task's `claude`
calls cost*. Choices the card bound to this task: `docs/decisions.md` ADR 67
(figure formats; `call_kinds` and `call` printed verbatim).

## What was built

- `front/src/lib/api/types.ts`: `UsageLine` and `UsageGroup`, annotations of
  ADR 54's block, guarded where they render.
- `client.ts`: `listUsage(taskId)` through `readEnvelope`; no mapping, sort or
  sum. `queries.ts`: `usageQuery`, key `["usage", taskId]`, `POLL_MS * 2`.
- `forward.ts`: `TASK_USAGE`, its own match tested before `TASK_DETAIL`, no
  query parameter copied. The route count prose moved seven → eight in
  `forward.ts`, `client.ts`, `front/AGENTS.md`, `front/README.md` and
  `docs/README.md`.
- `format.ts`: `formatCount`, `formatUsd`, `formatMs` (ADR 67).
- `routes/tasks.$taskId.tsx`: `UsageRegion` directly under the phase timeline,
  titled *what this task's calls cost*. Its warnings join the page's one banner.

## How it reads

- States, in order: loading draws nothing in the region; a failed read is the
  region's `ErrorState`; `null` is an `ErrorState` carrying the served
  warnings, never the empty state; a non-list is `Malformed`; `[]` is the
  empty state; rows are groups.
- Groups are bucketed by `(role, round)`; a bucket of n > 1 labels each group
  `attempt k of n`, and a group whose `account` differs from the previous one
  shows `failover from <account>` with how the earlier attempt's last line ended.
- A group prints account, `call_kinds`, `call_count`, `N unmeasured`, the first
  call (`first call; N retries not included`) and `calls (n)` in a closed
  `Collapsible`. Every line's cost is headed *session cost so far*.
- `phase_call === null` says `no phase line in this attempt`; a line with
  `measured === false` says `no result: crashed or timed out` instead of its
  figures.

## The arithmetic the card asks for

Two subtractions: retries not included (`call_count - 1`) and lines whose
`measured` is neither (`call_count - measured_calls - unmeasured_calls`). Both
count lines, not tokens or dollars, so they do not break "the console computes
nothing". No sum, no difference of costs, nothing across groups.

## Ruled out

- A per-attempt or per-task total: no key is served, and ADR 64 says the
  console does not make one.
- Relabelling `gate-retry` and friends: ADR 67.
- A fixture under `mock/`: nothing there returns usage.
