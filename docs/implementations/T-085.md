# T-085 — Tags, rank and the Ready order (board-service step 5c)

Decisions: `docs/decisions.md` ADR 130. It covers:
- `tags`, a free list checked on read and normalized on write;
- `rank`, a sparse integer inside the priority bucket;
- the *Ready* order and its dependency rule, as pure functions;
- what `board_list` serves.

## What was built

- `dispatcher/context_transfer.py`: `TaskFile.tags` and `TaskFile.rank`.
  They are read from the frontmatter and written only when set.
- `dispatcher/tasks.py`:
  - The constants `RANK_STEP` (1024), `MAX_RANK` (`2**53 - 1`), `MAX_TAGS`
    (20) and `MAX_TAG_CHARS` (50).
  - `tag_problems` and `normal_tags`; `field_problems` now reports a bad
    `tags` or `rank`.
  - `bucket_of`, `rank_of` (a missing or bad rank is 0) and `order_key`,
    which sorts by bucket, rank and the id in natural order.
  - `effective_buckets` and `ready_order`, the Ready order, and
    `read_all_tasks`.
  - `_end_of_bucket`: the highest rank in a bucket plus one step, capped at
    `MAX_RANK`, read under `CREATE_LOCK`.
  - `propose` takes `tags` and gives a rank at the end of the bucket; an
    epic's tasks take the epic's rank plus `k` steps and its tags.
    `_propose_again` gives the same to the tasks it finishes.
  - `edit_task` takes `tags` and `rank`. A new `priority` without a `rank`
    moves the task to the end of the new bucket. It takes `CREATE_LOCK` when
    the title, `priority` or `rank` is given.
  - `accept` and `_accept_epic` give the end of the bucket to a task whose
    bucket changes. `accept_drafts` ranks the drafts in order.
- `board_service/app.py`:
  - `board_list`: `tags` and `rank` per row; the backlog rows by bucket,
    rank and id after the board rows by id; a top-level `ready`.
  - The schemas: `tags` on `backlog_propose` and `task_edit`, `rank` on
    `task_edit`. `_check` enforces `minimum` and `maximum`.
  - The tool descriptions name the new arguments.
- Tests:
  - `tests/dispatcher/test_board_order.py`, new, 41 tests: tags, ranks,
    concurrent proposals, rebucketing, and the Ready order over chains,
    diamonds, epics, cycles and bad fields.
  - `tests/board_service/test_order.py`, new, 20 tests: the schemas, the
    refusals, list and get, the order, `ready` under filters, and HTTP.
  - `tests/dispatcher/test_board_writes.py` and
    `tests/board_service/test_app.py`: updated for the new `changed` entry
    and the new row fields.
- Full suite: 2964 passed, 10 skipped.

## Why this way

- A sparse rank moves one item with one write, and the integer stays exact
  in JSON.
- The end of a bucket is read and written under `CREATE_LOCK`, the lock the
  writers already take first, so concurrent proposals get distinct ranks.
- The effective bucket only raises, so an epic never demotes its own task,
  and a proposal never reorders the board.
- `ready` sits in `board_list`, which already reads every task; no new tool,
  no new role row.
