# T-150 — verdicts name the signed-in person

Carries out ADR 384 in `docs/decisions.md`; resolves `docs/debt/T-146-D2.md`.

## What

- `board_service/app.py`: `_person()` returns `g.session["user"]` when the
  request carries a live session (`hasattr(g, "session")`, set by
  `signin.check_session`), else `OPERATOR`.
- `_review_action` (approve, request-changes) passes `_person()` to
  `task_store.record_verdict` as the author. The `actor="operator"` of the
  queued action is unchanged.
- `_http` passes `_person()` as the role for `task_comment` and
  `review_verdict` only; every other console write stays `OPERATOR`.
- `tests/board_service/test_verdict_author.py`: an owner `ana` is the author of
  approve, request-changes, comment verdict and comment; `review_comments`
  pairs her comment with her request-changes verdict; no cookie still writes
  `operator`; a reviewer's comment names the reviewer and its approve is 403.

## Why this way

Comments change with verdicts because `dispatcher/tasks.py` `review_comments`
pairs a request-changes verdict with comments by the same `author`.

## Ruled out

- Building the reviewer's *Awaiting owner's approval* state: not this debt.
- Accept/drop `by`, proposal `source` and action `actor`: their readers expect
  role names (ADR 384).

## Notes

No effect over the console until the forward sends the session cookie
(`docs/plans/server-deployment.md` step 2); it is proven by the tests. A
session test sends the cookie, `X-CSRF-Token` and the bearer together. Each
approve needs its own task: a pending approve action makes a second one 409.
