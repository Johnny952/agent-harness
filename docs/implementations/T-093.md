# T-093 — The console's Backlog screen is real

Decisions: `docs/decisions.md` ADR 138. It covers:
- the one read, `/api/tasks`, filtered to the backlog statuses;
- the board's order, copied from `dispatcher/tasks.py:order_key`;
- epic grouping, the verdicts and their bodies, and the reorder's rank rule;
- the filters, the toast, and the removal of the mock.

## What was built

- `front/src/lib/api/types.ts`: `Task` gains `epic`, `split_into`
  (`unknown`), `merged` and `title`, the eighteen keys of ADR 137.
- `front/src/lib/backlog.ts`, the screen's arithmetic as plain functions:
  - `bucketOf`, `rankOf`, `compareIds` and `compareTasks`: the board's order.
  - `splitInto` and `isEpic`.
  - `groupBacklog`: an epic's backlog tasks under its id, with its row as
    the head when served.
  - `matches` and `filterGroups`: status, tag and source, keeping a group
    whose head or any child matches.
  - `isReorderable` and `reorderWrites`: the midpoint, or a renumber that
    writes only the changed ranks.
- `front/src/routes/backlog.tsx`, rewritten:
  - Rows show id (to `/tasks/$taskId`), title, priority, tags, source (a
    debt item's `debt_id` to `/debt`) and status.
  - Accept with a priority select, defer, drop with a confirm, through
    `acceptTaskMutation`; up and down through `editTask` with `{rank}`.
  - Status checkboxes, tag and source selects; empty and no-match states.
  - Errors and successes in a `sonner` toast.
- Mock removed: `listBacklog` and `setBacklogCompleted` (`client.ts`),
  `backlogQuery` (`queries.ts`), `BacklogItem` and its kinds
  (`ops-types.ts`), `initialBacklog` (`mock/ops-fixtures.ts`).
- `front/src/lib/api/mock/fixtures.ts`, `front/src/routes/-index.test.tsx`
  and `front/src/routes/-tasks.test.tsx`: the four new fields.
- Tests:
  - `front/src/routes/-backlog.test.tsx` (16): order; dropped hidden by
    default; tag, source and `none`; no-match state; the debt and task
    links; accept with priority, defer, drop after confirm, each body; a
    refusal in a toast; reorder by midpoint and by renumber; epic grouping
    and a child's accept held; the empty state.
  - `front/src/lib/backlog.test.ts` (8): ids, ranks, `splitInto`,
    `reorderWrites` and `groupBacklog`.
- `bun run typecheck`, `bun run test` (252 passed, 15 files) and
  `bun run lint` pass.
- `docs/README.md` and `front/README.md`: the Backlog is no longer a mock.

## Why this way

- The order is copied rule for rule so the screen never shows an order the
  board will not keep.
- The rank is computed over the whole bucket so a filter never moves a row
  the operator cannot see.
- Native selects and buttons, as on the board: no new dependency, and they
  work in jsdom.

## Pending

- A renumber is several `PATCH`es; a refusal mid-way leaves the earlier
  ones written. The next read shows the result.
- An unranked item sorts first in its bucket, as the dispatcher sorts it;
  the screen says nothing more about it.
- Editing a backlog item's title or tags, and proposing one, are not on this
  screen.

## Compose

No change.

## Ruled out

- A drag-and-drop library, renumbering on every move, moving dropped rows
  and parallel rank writes (ADR 138).
