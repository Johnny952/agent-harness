# T-070 — the `command_requests` handoff field, its check, and the `command_request` block (command-requests step 2)

Decisions: `docs/decisions.md` ADR 117 (the filing roles, the shape and its
limit of 3, the check's refusal reasons, one accepted request blocks, no gates
or revisor after it, `blocked_reason` and where it is cleared, the envelope's
sixth key, the budget raise, the prompt paragraph).

## What was built

- `dispatcher/handoff.py`: `_COMMAND_REQUESTS_PROPERTY` (at most 3 entries of
  `argv`, `reason`, `target{kind, name}`, `cwd`) on the cartografo's and the
  implementador's `_ROLE_EXTRAS`; their budgets +768 bytes (3328 and 7936).
  `command_request_problems(entry)` names every problem of one entry;
  `command_requests(payload)` answers `(accepted, refused)`, refusing past the
  third "over the limit of 3 requests"; `files_command_requests(role)`;
  `COMMAND_REQUESTS_PROMPT`; `COMMAND_REQUEST`, the block reason. `render`
  gains a *Command requests* section.
- `dispatcher/context_transfer.py`: `TaskFile.blocked_reason`, read from and
  written to the frontmatter only when set; `COMMAND_REQUESTS_ABSENT` and
  `save_handoff(..., command_requests=...)`, the envelope's sixth key.
- `dispatcher/tasks.py`: `mark_blocked(..., reason=None)` sets the reason (no
  reason clears it); `acquire_lock` clears it.
- `dispatcher/dispatcher.py`: `_role_prompt` adds the paragraph for the two
  roles; `run_phase` records both lists for them; `_run_gates` returns before
  grading on an accepted request; `_filed_command_requests` logs refusals and
  answers whether to block, called after the implementador and in both mapping
  branches, ending the cycle with `blocked(handoff.COMMAND_REQUEST)`;
  `_mark_blocked` and the `blocked` closure pass the reason on.
- Tests: `tests/dispatcher/test_command_requests.py` — the field on the two
  roles and on no other; each kind accepted; each refusal reason; every
  problem named; more than 3; an empty list, a missing field and a non-list;
  the rendered section; the envelope's lists read back and no key for a role
  that cannot file; an implementador request ending Blocked with the reason
  and no revisor; all-refused not blocking; the same two for mapping; the
  prompt paragraph only for the filing roles; the task file's key absent
  unless set, cleared by `acquire_lock` and by a block with no reason;
  `_run_gates` not grading. `tests/dispatcher/test_handoff.py` (the margin
  test's cartografo size, 300 to 340) and `tests/dispatcher/test_parallel_queue.py`
  (the `_mark_blocked` stub takes `reason`) follow the changes.
- Docs: ADR 117; the *Built* note under decision 2 and the dated answer under
  "Which roles may file" in `docs/plans/command-requests.md`.

## Why this way

- The envelope, not a separate file: it is already the phase's one record,
  written atomically with the return, and a second file could drift from it.
- The check is pure and lives in `handoff.py` with the schema, so the
  cycle, the gates and the envelope all apply the same verdict.
- The branch sits right after the phase returns: the implementador's before
  the revisor, the mapper's before the arquitecto, after the foreign-lock
  check so a cycle that lost its lock does not write a block.
- `blocked_reason` is cleared in `acquire_lock`, the one place a task starts
  running again, rather than at each caller that releases it.
- All-refused does not block: a malformed request is not a reason to stop a
  phase that may have finished; the warning and the record are enough.

## Pending

- The CLI's enforcement of `maxItems` on `--json-schema` is not verified
  (no model calls in this task); the check refuses past 3 either way.
- A blank `reason`, a blank `cwd` and a `name` on a non-service target are
  accepted.
- The plan's later steps (target resolution and the denylist, verdicts,
  running and resuming, the allowlist, the Approvals card) are not started.

## Ruled out

- A field on the arquitecto, the revisor or the auditor.
- Blocking on refused requests.
- Running the gates or the revisor after a filed request.
