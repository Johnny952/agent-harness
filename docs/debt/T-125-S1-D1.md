# T-125-S1-D1 — `approve_reviewed` calls the private `docker_exec._rev_parse`

**Origin:** introduced, declared by T-125-S1's arquitecto and implementador.
**Status:** resolved 2026-10-21 by T-126 (`docker_exec.resolve_commit`).
**Ruling:** accepted by the revisor in round 1.
**Card:** none — nothing mirrors a row (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 59).
**Impact:** risk.

## What

`dispatcher/dispatcher.py` `_stale_reason`, the stale check of
`approve_reviewed` (ADR 258, 6; ADR 264), resolves the task branch's tip and
the reviewed commit through `docker_exec._rev_parse`, a private helper of
`dispatcher/docker_exec.py`. Tests monkeypatch it, since `fake_git` does not
fake it.

## Where it bites

A task that touches `dispatcher/docker_exec.py` `_rev_parse` (renames it,
changes its signature or what it returns for a missing ref), or that adds a
second caller outside `docker_exec.py`.

## Why it stayed

`dispatcher/docker_exec.py` was outside T-125-S1's surface.

## Cost of leaving it

A rename or a changed return in `docker_exec` breaks the approve path's stale
check; the approve tests monkeypatch the name, so they may keep passing while
the real call fails.

## Fix

Add a public `docker_exec.resolve_commit` (the same `rev-parse --verify
<rev>^{commit}` read) and switch `dispatcher._stale_reason` and its tests to it.
