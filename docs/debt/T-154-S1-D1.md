# T-154-S1-D1 — `dispatcher/queue.py` does not pass `start_at`, `start_round` or `on_held` yet

**Origin:** introduced, by T-154-S1; declared by its arquitecto and implementador.
**Status:** resolved 2026-10-23 by T-154-S2.
**Ruling:** accepted by T-154-S1's revisor.
**Card:** none — nothing mirrors a row (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 59).
**Impact:** cost.

## What

Since T-154-S1 `run_task_cycle` in `dispatcher/dispatcher.py` takes
`start_at`, `start_round` and `on_held` (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 354): a
cycle can enter at a round's implementador off the saved arquitecto envelope,
and a HELD cycle reports the (role, round) it was held at. Nothing calls it
that way yet: the queue runners in `dispatcher/queue.py` neither record what
`on_held` reports nor pass it back on a resume, so `T-152-D1` is only half
closed.

## Where it bites

A `run-queue --resume` of a `limit_reached` task that was held after its
arquitecto phase, or a task that changes how `dispatcher/queue.py` calls
`run_task_cycle`.

## Why it stayed

The queue and the CLI are outside this sub-task's surface (the
`run_task_cycle` contract); T-154's next sub-task wires them.

## Cost of leaving it

Each such resumed task still spends a second arquitecto phase, as `T-152-D1`
describes, until the queue passes the held phase back.

## Fix

The queue records `on_held`'s (role, round) on the entry and passes them back
as `start_at`/`start_round` on the resumed run (ADR 354; ADR 336, decision 5).
