# T-191-D1 — Settings `role_model` hint says an unset role runs on `default_model`

**Origin:** found, by T-191; declared by its arquitecto and implementador.
**Ruling:** accepted by T-191's revisor.
**Card:** none — nothing mirrors a row (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 59).
**Impact:** cosmetic.

## What

`front/src/routes/models.tsx` shows one hint under every `role_model` row:
"(default) — an empty field — takes the role out of the map, and it runs on
default_model." That is wrong for the two roles with a fallback:
`integrador` runs on the implementador's model and `arquitecto-draft` on the
arquitecto's (`dispatcher/config.py` `Config.model_for`; ADR 277 decision 4).
T-191 put the `integrador` row on the screen (ADR 507), so the wrong hint now
shows for two rows.

## Where it bites

A task that changes the `role_model` hint or rows in
`front/src/routes/models.tsx`, or changes the fallback in
`dispatcher/config.py` `Config.model_for`.

## Why it stayed

A screen change is a second surface; T-191's done-when is met by the api's
role list, which the screen renders as is.

## Cost of leaving it

An operator reads that an unset `integrador` or `arquitecto-draft` runs on
`default_model` when it runs on the implementador's or arquitecto's model.

## Fix

A per-role hint naming the fallback (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 507
*Consequences*, ADR 277 decision 4).
