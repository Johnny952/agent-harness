# T-024-D2 — whether a `--resume` result's `usage` is per call or cumulative over the session was never probed

**Origin:** introduced, declared by T-024's implementador in round 1 and again
in round 2, about the three `--resume` retries whose results
`docs/decisions.md` **ADR 53** now records as their own lines in
`<hive_tasks_dir>/<task_id>/usage/calls.jsonl`.
**Ruling:** accepted by the revisor in both rounds, on the ground that T-024's
card forbade a live probe and no stored CLI result object exists anywhere on
this harness, so the only alternative to deferring was a guessed number — which
is the one thing ADR 53 forbids on this log. Round 1 required the *Fix* to stop
asking a later task to write the answer into ADR 53, since
`docs/decisions.md`'s preamble forbids rewriting an entry already there
([an-adr-is-never-where-a-deferred-answer-lands](../learnings/an-adr-is-never-where-a-deferred-answer-lands.md));
round 2 accepted the redirected fix below.
**Card:** none — this harness has no board configured, the first reason given
in [`README.md`](README.md)'s closing paragraphs.

## What

A phase is up to four `claude` calls: the attempt itself, and one `--resume`
each from `_run_gates`, `_refuse_review_writes` and `_shrink_over_budget`
([a-phase-is-not-one-claude-call](../learnings/a-phase-is-not-one-claude-call.md)).
ADR 53 records each as its own line, which is right either way. What is unknown
is what the `usage` block on a **resumed** call counts:

- **Per call.** The retry's `input_tokens` is the tokens that one turn sent,
  and a phase's cost is the sum of its lines.
- **Cumulative over the session.** The retry's `input_tokens` includes the
  phase's own, because a resumed session re-sends its context, and a phase's
  cost is the *last* line, or a diff between consecutive ones.

Nothing on the branch assumes either. Every line is recorded as the CLI gave
it, nothing is summed, and ADR 53 says so explicitly. The debt is the missing
answer, not a wrong one in the code.

## Where it bites

A task that **sums or averages** the lines of one phase — an aggregator, a
median, a per-phase total, a budget — rather than reading them one call at a
time. `T-024-D1` is that task, and this question has to be answered before its
sum is written.

Not a task that reads a single line, reports one call, or counts calls: those
are correct under both answers. Not a task reading only `call: "phase"` lines
either, since a new session's first call has nothing to accumulate.

## Why it stays

T-024's card said in as many words not to run a probe or `/usage` against a
live container, and there is no stored CLI result object anywhere on this
harness to read the answer off instead
([no-stored-claude-result-object-exists-on-this-harness](../learnings/no-stored-claude-result-object-exists-on-this-harness.md)).
`docs/ROADMAP.md` **V1.1**, the check whose *Record* step would have written
the key set and the `usage` breakdown down, has no Results row — it was never
run.

So the alternatives were to guess, or to defer. A guessed answer here is worse
than an absent one in the specific way ADR 53 is built to prevent: it would
look like a measurement.

## Cost of leaving it

If `usage` is cumulative and an aggregator sums, a phase that took all three
retries double-counts its own context up to three times, and every median built
on it overstates — in the direction that makes the harness look more expensive
than it is, which is the direction that would drive a wrong sizing decision in
P4.

Bounded: it only bites a reader that sums, and there is no reader at all
(`T-024-D1`), so nothing is wrong today. The first aggregator is the first
thing that can be wrong, which is why this row is the precondition on that one.

## Fix

Dispatch one real phase that takes a retry — any role whose gate retry or
shrink retry fires — then read the two lines its account left in that task's
log and compare their `input_tokens`:

- the retry's is **larger** than the `call: "phase"` line's, by roughly the
  phase's own input → cumulative, and a reader must diff consecutive lines
  rather than sum them;
- the retry's is **small and independent** of it → per call, and a sum is
  correct.

`raw_keys` on both lines says at the same time whether `usage` was there at all
under the CLI version that ran, which is the other half of what V1.1 would have
recorded.

Write the answer in a **new** ADR narrowing ADR 53, or in the implementation
note of whichever task probes it — not into ADR 53, which is append-only once
merged. A later task that serves this log can do the probe as its first step
rather than as a separate task; the two are cheapest taken together.
