# T-152-D1 — No entry into a task cycle at a later phase; a `limit_reached` queue task re-runs from the arquitecto

**Origin:** introduced, by T-152; declared by its arquitecto and implementador.
**Status:** open.
**Ruling:** accepted by T-152's revisor.
**Card:** none — nothing mirrors a row (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 59).
**Impact:** cost.

## What

Since T-152 a `run-queue --resume QUEUE_ID --limit TOKENS` that leaves the
queue's spend below its new limit puts every task the queue recorded
`limit_reached` back to `pending` (`dispatcher/queue.py` `reopen`). Its cycle
is then `run_task_cycle` as for any task, which always starts at the
arquitecto on the task's branch as it stands. The task's done-when asked for
such a task to go on "from its saved phase"; `docs/decisions.md` ADR 336,
decision 5, narrows that, because no entry into a cycle at a later phase
exists.

## Where it bites

A resumed `run-queue` whose `limit_reached` task had already passed its
arquitecto phase before the limit stopped it — or a task that adds a
start-at-phase entry to `run_task_cycle` in `dispatcher/dispatcher.py`.

## Why it stayed

The entry point belongs to `dispatcher/dispatcher.py`'s cycle, outside
T-152's surface (`dispatcher/queue.py`, `dispatcher/cli.py`, their tests and
docs).

## Cost of leaving it

Each such task spends a second arquitecto phase on every limit-raising resume,
and the queue's confirmation does not estimate that spend (ADR 336,
*Consequences*).

## Fix

Add a `start_at` role (with its round state) to `run_task_cycle` in
`dispatcher/dispatcher.py`, then have the queue pass the phase the task was
held at; ADR 336, decision 5, names the gap.
