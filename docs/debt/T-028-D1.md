# T-028-D1 — `front/` still declares a task's card and `kanban_issue_id`

**Origin:** introduced, declared by T-028's arquitecto and by the
implementador in rounds 1 and 2.
**Ruling:** accepted by the revisor in both rounds: the card puts `front/` out
of scope and no screen reads either field.
**Card:** none — nothing mirrors a row (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 59).

## What

Since T-028 the api's task routes serve neither `card` nor `kanban_issue_id`.
The console still declares both:

- `front/src/lib/api/types.ts` keeps the `KanbanIssue` interface, whose
  docblock names the deleted client module, and `Task` keeps
  `kanban_issue_id` and `card`;
- the fixtures under `front/src/lib/api/mock/` still return them;
- `front/src/routes/debt.tsx` has a comment describing the debt index's
  `card` cell as the id of a card on the Kanban board, and the docblock on
  the debt row's `card` field in `types.ts` says the same.

## Where it bites

A task editing `Task` in `front/src/lib/api/types.ts` or the task fixtures,
or rendering a task's card. Also a task that changes what the debt index's
`card` column holds (step 5 of `docs/plans/board-service.md`), which has to
reword the `debt.tsx` comment. Not a task on a screen that reads neither
field, which is every screen today.

## Why it stays

T-028's card puts `front/` out of scope, behind its own ask for a build.

## Cost of leaving it

Stale annotations. A later screen could read a field that is always
`undefined`, and `tsc` would not object, because the type still promises it
(`docs/learnings/a-console-type-over-a-served-value-is-an-annotation.md`).

## Fix

One `front/` task removes `KanbanIssue`, the two `Task` fields, the fixture
values and the two comments, per ADR 59 *Consequences*. The fixture edit is
part of the same commit
(`docs/learnings/narrowing-a-served-type-is-also-a-fixture-edit.md`).
