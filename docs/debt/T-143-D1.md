# T-143-D1 — `operator.py` counts a refusal's cooldown from `quota_cooldown_seconds` and ignores `retry_at`

**Origin:** introduced, by T-143; declared by its arquitecto and implementador.
**Status:** resolved 2026-10-22 by T-145 (`operator.py` reads `_refusal_hold_until`, ADR 318).
**Ruling:** accepted by T-143's revisor.
**Card:** none — nothing mirrors a row (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 59).
**Impact:** risk.

## What

Since T-143 a phase refused as rate-limited records the `resetsAt` of its
`rate_limit_event` as `retry_at` beside `rate_limited_at`
(`dispatcher/state_machine.py` `record_rate_limit`, `get_retry_at`), and the
dispatcher holds the account until then (`dispatcher/dispatcher.py`
`_refusal_hold_until`, `docs/decisions.md` ADR 312 point 6).
`dispatcher/operator.py` was outside the card's Surface and still measures the
hold as `rate_limited_at + quota_cooldown_seconds` through
`_cooldown_remaining`, in two places:

- the cooldown-left `account_reports` computes and `format_status` prints;
- `release_account`'s refusal guard and its message ("… of its Ns cooldown is
  left").

## Where it bites

A task touching `dispatcher/operator.py` `_cooldown_remaining`, the status
text, or `release-account`'s cooldown guard; or any operator relying on
`dispatch status` / `release-account` for an account refused with a
`resetsAt` further away than `quota_cooldown_seconds`.

## Why it stayed

`operator.py` was outside T-143's Surface; the dispatcher itself honours
`retry_at`.

## Cost of leaving it

Status shows a wrong cooldown-left for such an account. Found by T-143's
auditor, beyond the text the revisor ruled on: once `quota_cooldown_seconds`
has passed but `retry_at` has not, `release-account` no longer refuses, so an
operator release can queue the account straight back into a 429 — the very
thing that guard exists to stop.

## Fix

Have `operator._cooldown_remaining` read `state_machine.get_retry_at` and use
it when present, the same rule as `dispatcher._refusal_hold_until` (ADR 312
point 6), and word both messages as "until <time>" rather than a fixed
cooldown.
