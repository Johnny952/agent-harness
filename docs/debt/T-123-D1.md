# T-123-D1 — `docs/plans/autonomy.md` still bounds `--parallel` at one phase per account

**Origin:** found, declared by T-123's arquitecto and implementador.
**Status:** resolved 2026-10-21 by T-124 (see *How it was resolved*).
**Ruling:** accepted by the revisor in round 1.
**Card:** none — nothing mirrors a row (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 59).
**Impact:** cosmetic.

## What

`docs/plans/autonomy.md`, B-3's bullet *Slots still bound it.*, says that with
two accounts there are two slots and an account holds one phase. Since T-123,
`run-queue --parallel N` is capped at the pool's phase slots, the sum of each
account's `max_concurrent_phases` (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 252, amending ADR 83
decision 1); ADR 88 already let an account hold several phases.

## Where it bites

A task editing `docs/plans/autonomy.md` B-3 or *Decisions* 8, or any text
describing the `--parallel` ceiling, or a reader of that plan sizing
`--parallel` for a pool with `max_concurrent_phases` above 1.

## Why it stayed

The file was outside T-123's named surface (`dispatcher/cli.py`,
`dispatcher/queue.py`, the tests, `docs/decisions.md`, `docs/plans/projects.md`).

## Cost of leaving it

A reader of the plan sees the old ceiling; `dispatcher/cli.py` `_run_queue` and
ADR 252 are the correction.

## Fix

Add a one-line pointer to `docs/decisions.md` ADR 252 next to the *Slots still
bound it.* text, saying the ceiling is now the sum of `max_concurrent_phases`.

## How it was resolved

T-124 took the fix as declared. The bullet sits in `docs/plans/autonomy.md`
*Decisions* 6, the decision B-3 builds, not under B-3 itself; its original
sentence stays as the record of what was decided then, and a new sentence
after it says that since T-123 the ceiling is the sum of each account's
`max_concurrent_phases` (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 252). *Decisions* 8 is left
as written: B-4's *Built* note already records its outcome.
