# T-150-D1 — Accept/drop `by`, a proposal's `source` and the action `actor` still record `operator` for a signed-in person

**Origin:** introduced, by T-150; declared by its arquitecto and implementador.
**Status:** open.
**Ruling:** accepted by T-150's revisor.
**Card:** none — nothing mirrors a row (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 59).
**Impact:** cost.

## What

T-150 made verdicts and comments written over HTTP name the signed-in person
(`docs/decisions.md` ADR 384, `board_service/app.py` `_person`). The other
console writes still pass `OPERATOR`: `backlog_accept`'s and
`backlog_drop`'s `by` (stored as `dropped_by`), a proposal's `source`, and the
`actor` of the actions `board_service/app.py` queues through `actions.create`
(the approve and request-changes actions included).

## Where it bites

A task that changes `backlog_accept` or `backlog_drop` over HTTP, a proposal's
`source`, or the `actor` passed to `actions.create` in `board_service/app.py`.

## Why it stayed

Outside the verdict surface `T-146-D2` named, and their readers expect role
names, not people (ADR 384, *Ruled out*).

## Cost of leaving it

Who accepted, dropped or started a task is only in the sign-in audit log,
joined by time and path, not on the record itself.

## Fix

Pass `_person()` as `by`/`source`/`actor` the way ADR 384 does for `author`,
after checking every reader of those fields copes with a person's name.
