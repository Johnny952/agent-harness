# T-146-D2 — Verdicts still record `operator` as their author, not the signed-in user

**Origin:** introduced, by T-146; declared by its arquitecto and implementador.
**Status:** open.
**Ruling:** accepted by T-146's revisor.
**Card:** none — nothing mirrors a row (`docs/decisions.md` ADR 59).
**Impact:** cost.

## What

Since T-146 a person signs in and the board service knows who made each write
(`board_service/signin.py`, the `action` audit line). The verdict a task
records still names `operator` as its author: the board service's verdict
route in `board_service/app.py` passes the `OPERATOR` constant as the `author`
of `task_store.record_verdict`, not the session's user.

## Where it bites

A task that changes `record_verdict` or the review verdict route, or builds the
reviewer's *Awaiting owner's approval* state of
`docs/plans/server-deployment.md` *Decisions* 2.

## Why it stayed

*Decisions* 2's `by` field changes the verdict format, which is outside step
1's surface (the users store, sessions, CSRF and audit log).

## Cost of leaving it

Who gave a verdict is only in the audit log, joined by time and path, not on
the task itself; two people's verdicts read the same on the task.

## Fix

Pass the session's user into `task_store.record_verdict` when the reviewer
flow is built (`docs/plans/server-deployment.md` *Decisions* 2).
